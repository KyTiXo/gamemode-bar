GameModeBar

macOS Game Mode, on your terms

A native menu-bar toggle that forces Game Mode and turns off AirDrop for emulators, streaming, and AirPlay. No Electron, and it stays out of your way.

1. Install

curl -fsSL https://kytixo.github.io/gamemode-bar/install.sh | sh

Beta builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized. On first launch, right-click the app and choose Open.

2. Authorize and play

Open the controller icon in your menu bar and choose Settings… → Check Permissions…. It checks for full Xcode (which provides gamepolicyctl) and installs one sudoers rule that only allows ifconfig awdl0 up and down.

Then click Enable Game Mode+. Click it again to put everything back.

When it helps

AirPlay to a TV

AWDL traffic competes with the same Wi‑Fi. Turning off AirDrop can smooth out the stream.

Emulators and streaming

shadPS4, Moonlight, and other full-screen play that macOS doesn't treat as games.

Anything on a controller

Apple's automatic Game Mode is hit-or-miss. This forces the same policy path.

Results vary by Mac, network, and workload. It's a policy toggle, not an FPS patch.
